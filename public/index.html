IdleProxy

You pay for a coding-agent subscription. Most hours it sits idle. IdleProxy meters that capacity out to agents that pay per call — settled onchain through KeeperHub.

Before you connect: relaying your own subscription to strangers likely violates your provider's resale terms, and this demo runs on testnet — payouts have no monetary value. Full disclosure is shown before you accept anything below.
