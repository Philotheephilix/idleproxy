# Users and Organizations

KeeperHub provides multi-level access management for individuals, teams, and organizations.

-   [Users](https://docs.keeperhub.com/users-teams-orgs/users) — Account management and personal settings
-   [Teams](https://docs.keeperhub.com/users-teams-orgs/teams) — Team creation and collaboration
-   [Organizations](https://docs.keeperhub.com/users-teams-orgs/organizations) — Organization-level management
-   [Permissions](https://docs.keeperhub.com/users-teams-orgs/permissions) — Role-based access control
